About UTM parameters

UTM parameters are small tags added to the end of a link. They don't change where it goes. When someone clicks, your analytics tool reads them and files the visit under the right campaign.

  • utm_source: who sent the visitor, e.g. newsletter, linkedin.
  • utm_medium: the kind of channel, e.g. email, social, cpc.
  • utm_campaign: the initiative, e.g. spring_sale.
  • utm_id, utm_term, utm_content: optional. An ID to match your records, the paid-search keyword, and a label to tell similar links apart.

Tip: use lowercase everywhere. LinkedIn and linkedin show up as two different sources in reports.
